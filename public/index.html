Accounts

Pooled, not minted on demand

Virtual accounts are pre-provisioned and assigned to a customer, matching how real dedicated virtual account rails work.

Trust

Only a signed webhook credits you

No endpoint lets a client or merchant mark a payment successful directly — that's the one path an attacker can't fake.

Ledger

Double-entry, idempotent

Every credit posts a balanced ledger pair. Retried webhooks and race conditions can't double-credit a wallet.

Integration, top to bottom
POST /api/customers → register the buyer POST /api/virtual-accounts → assign an account for this order (bank webhook lands, wallet is credited) POST your /webhook ← RexxPay pushes "transaction.success" signed with your webhookSecret
