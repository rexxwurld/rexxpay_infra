Settlement schedule

How a confirmed payment moves to your payout-ready balance
ℹ
Settlement batches move a transaction through pending_settlement → settled → available on a schedule, but that history is only exposed through an operator-only endpoint (/api/v1/admin/settlement/batches, gated by INFRA_ADMIN_KEY) — not a merchant-scoped one yet. Your wallet balance on the Overview tab already reflects settled funds; a per-transaction settlement timeline for merchants is the next thing to add on the backend before this panel can show real data.
